            Sunday Evening

            Dinner: Miso-Maple Chicken, Smashed Potatoes & Greens

            Assembly: Roast chicken #1 skin-up at 200°C for 45-50 min. Smash 1kg baby potatoes with oil and salt, roast below chicken 35 min until crisp. Reheat 650g greens last 8 min. Rest, carve, serve with salsa verde and pea dip.

            Dairy-Free Swap: Tahini sauce.
            Optional Dairy Finisher: Shaved parmesan on potatoes.

            Next-Day Lunch Rescue

            Chicken wraps in flatbreads with pea dip and cos + 2 egg muffins.

            Toddler Snack Moment

            Quartered unsalted egg muffins, halved sweet potato wedges.

            Monday Evening

            Dinner: Ginger-Garlic Tamari Steak Bowls

            Assembly: Sear marinated steak 2.5 min/side in a ripping hot skillet, rest 8 min, slice thin against the grain. Serve over reheated coconut jasmine rice with pickles, greens, sesame-nori sprinkle, miso glaze drizzle, and sweet potato wedges.

            Dairy-Free Swap: Salsa verde.
            Optional Dairy Finisher: Butter knob on steak.

            Next-Day Lunch Rescue

            Cold steak bowl with rice, pickles, 2 jammy eggs.

            After-School Hunger Hour

            2 egg muffins, 1 oat bar, shrub fizz.

            Tuesday Evening

            Dinner: Basil-Zucchini Meatball Rice Bowls

            Assembly: Simmer 28 pre-baked meatballs in 900ml tomato sauce for 12 min. Serve over thawed coconut rice with cucumber-cos salad and sesame-nori sprinkle.

            Dairy-Free Swap: Fresh basil + salsa verde drizzle.
            Optional Dairy Finisher: Melted mozzarella.

            Next-Day Lunch Rescue

            Meatball wraps in 3 flatbreads with pea dip and cos.

            After-School Hunger Hour

            3 scrolls, fresh strawberries.

            Wednesday Evening

            Dinner: Kangaroo-Chicken Sliders

            Assembly: Reheat 18 slider patties (190°C 10 min), warm 5 flatbreads. Build sliders with tahini sauce, pickles, tomato confit, and cos. Serve with remaining 900g smashed potatoes.

            Midweek Refresh Note: Top up fruit boxes, blanch 1 bunch broccolini.
            Dairy-Free Swap: Tahini sauce & pepita crunch.
            Optional Dairy Finisher: Crumbled feta.

            Next-Day Lunch Rescue

            Slider wraps (6 patties in 3 flatbreads with tahini & pickles).

            After-School Hunger Hour

            3 energy balls, seed crunch, rockmelon cooler.

            Thursday Evening

            Dinner: Crisp Panko Katsu Curry

            Assembly: Bake 10 katsu cutlets from frozen (200°C 20-22 min until crisp). Reheat 600ml katsu-curry sauce, serve over thawed jasmine rice with pickles and sesame-nori sprinkle.

            Dairy-Free Swap: Naturally DF.
            Optional Dairy Finisher: None needed.

            Next-Day Lunch Rescue

            Katsu wraps with curry sauce, cos, cucumber.

            After-School Hunger Hour

            2 cookies, banana, shrub fizz.

            Friday Evening

            Dinner: Sticky Thigh Fried Rice

            Assembly: Cook frozen veg base in a hot wok 3 min. Add 1.05kg thawed thigh bites, 800g thawed rice. Scramble 6 eggs with 4 tbsp miso glaze into the wok. Finish with spring onion & sesame-nori.

            Note: Pull Saturday chicken/meatballs from freezer to thaw.

            Next-Day Lunch Rescue

            Egg muffins, leftover fried rice, fruit box.

            After-School Hunger Hour

            2 jammy eggs, flatbread w/ pea dip, 1 oat bar.

            Saturday Evening

            Dinner: Freezer-Raid Flatbread Pizza

            Assembly: Crisp 5 flatbreads in dry pan. Top with 300ml tomato sauce, 600g shredded chicken, 16 sliced meatballs, spinach, and tomato confit. Bake 220°C 6 min, top with pea dip dollop.

            Dairy-Free Swap: Pea dip dollop.
            Optional Dairy Finisher: Mozzarella cheese.

            Weekend Snack Deployment

            Strawberry-banana coconut-yoghurt bark straight from the freezer.

            Toddler Moments

            Pea-avocado mash on flatbread fingers, soft meatball halves.

            School & Snack Deployment Rules

            Standard School Lunch Formula:
            1 Savory (2 egg muffins / 2 scrolls / slider wrap) + 1 Sweet (1 oat bar / 2 energy balls) + 150g fruit box + cucumber sticks + frozen rooibos-mango iced tea bottle.
            Toddler Safe Modifications:
            Quartered unsalted egg muffins, warmed oat bar strips, halved sweet potato wedges, soft meatball halves, pea-avocado mash on flatbread fingers, quartered berries, jammy egg quarters.
