Linear Chronological Batch Workflow

Estimated 4.5–5 hrs wall-clock. Raw meat prep and coating is isolated strictly last before clean-up.

    Stage A: Long-Simmer Liquids, Grains & Pots

  • 1. Potatoes: cut 2.2kg into 5cm chunks. Boil in salted water 10 min. Drain, shake hard to rough edges, spread on a tray and steam-dry.
  • 2. Rice: rinse 700g basmati. Combine with 1.05L water, 1 tsp salt and 1 tbsp olive oil. Cook covered on low 12 min, then rest 10 min off heat. Fluff and spread on a tray.
  • 3. Maple-BBQ sauce: simmer 2 × 680g passata, 100ml ACV, 120ml maple, 2 tbsp Dijon, 1 tbsp mustard powder, 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp pepper, ¼ tsp ground cloves, 1.5 tsp salt and 200ml water for 30 min. Set aside 600ml for pork.
  • 4. Butter chicken sauce: sweat 3 diced onions, 6 garlic cloves and 50g grated ginger in 3 tbsp oil for 8 min. Add spices and tomato paste, cook 1 min. Add jar passata, 400ml coconut cream, 300ml stock, 1 tbsp maple and salt. Simmer 25 min.
  • 5. Gravy base: caramelise 3 sliced onions in 3 tbsp oil with 6 thyme sprigs and 1 tsp salt for 35 min. Stir in 40g flour for 2 min, add 800ml stock and 1 tbsp ACV. Simmer 15 min.
  • 6. Cauliflower sauce: simmer 1.3kg cauliflower, 2 diced onions and 8 garlic cloves in 600ml stock and 400ml coconut milk for 20 min until soft.
  • 7. Compote: simmer 900g halved strawberries, 200g blueberries, 3 maple, 2 tsp vanilla for 8 min. Stir in 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp water, cook 3 min. Cool.
  • 8. Black beans: sauté 1 diced onion and 3 garlic cloves in oil. Add 2 drained tins black beans, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp oregano, 150ml stock. Simmer 12 min, mash a quarter.
  • 9. Custard: whisk 40g cornflour, 50ml maple, 2 tsp vanilla, 100ml cold oat milk. Heat 600ml oat milk until steaming, whisk in slurry, stir 3–4 min until thick. Cover with cling film.
  • 10. Lemonade base: simmer 800g halved strawberries, 100ml maple, 400ml water for 10 min. Mash.

    • Stage B: Blender, Sauces & Dips

  • 11. Blend cauliflower sauce with 2 tbsp nutritional yeast, 1 tbsp Dijon, 2 tsp salt, pepper and 80ml olive oil until silky (~1.8L).
  • 12. Blend butter chicken sauce with 120g baby spinach until smooth. Return to pot.
  • 13. Blitz half the gravy base smooth, stir back through.
  • 14. Lemonade: strain strawberry mash into 4L jug. Juice 8 lemons (~400ml), add 2.8L cold water. Chill.
  • 15. Aioli: blend 2 eggs, 3 grated garlic cloves, 1 tbsp ACV, 1 tsp Dijon, 1 tsp salt. Slowly stream in 300ml light olive oil.
  • 16. Crema: whisk 400ml coconut cream (thick part) with 1 tbsp ACV, 1 grated garlic clove, ½ tsp salt, 2 tbsp water.
  • 17. Cannellini dip: simmer 6 garlic cloves in water 8 min. Blend with 2 tins cannellini, 80ml olive oil, 2 tbsp ACV, oregano, parsley, 100ml water, salt.
  • 18. Slaw dressing: whisk 120ml olive oil, 60ml ACV, 2 tbsp maple, 2 tsp Dijon, 1 tsp salt.

    • Stage C: Board Prep, Veg, Pickles & Baking Batters

  • 19. Pickles: slice 3 red onions and 4 cucumbers. Cover with hot brine (250ml ACV, 250ml water, sugar, salt, peppercorns).
  • 20. Slaw base: shred red cabbage, add 400g grated carrot and 1 bunch sliced spring onion. Keep undressed.
  • 21. Sticks: cut 800g carrot batons, 4 cucumbers into sticks, 300g snow peas. Pack separately.
  • 22. Sweet potato: cut 2.2kg into wedges (oil, salt, garlic powder, oregano on 2 trays). Dice 800g into 2cm cubes on tray 3.
  • 23. Corn: husk 8 cobs, oil and salt, lay on tray.
  • 24. Crumble mix: rub 320g cold butter into 450g flour, 400g oats, 220g raw sugar, cinnamon, salt. Clump 450g on tray for clusters; keep 950g in bowl for bars.
  • 25. Muffin batter: mash 5 bananas, whisk with 2 eggs, 150g melted butter, raw sugar, oat milk, vanilla. Fold in flour, baking powder, cinnamon, salt, choc chips. Fill 24 cases.
  • 26. Bar base: press 550g crumble mix into 20x30cm tin, spread 300g compote, top with remaining 400g crumble.
  • 27. Cookie dough: cream 250g butter and brown sugar, beat in egg and vanilla. Fold in flour, oats, baking powder, salt, choc chips. Scoop 36 balls, chill.
  • 28. Scone dough: rub 150g coconut oil into 900g flour, baking powder, salt, garlic powder. Add herbs/onions. Stir in oat milk + ACV. Cut 24 scones.
  • 29. Pikelets: mash 3 bananas, whisk with eggs, oat milk, vanilla. Fold in flour, baking powder, salt. Cook 40 pikelets in buttery pan.
  • 30. Start non-meat oven runs (Runs 1–7) immediately.

    • Stage D: Raw Meat Prep & Coating (Strictly Last)

  • 31. Wipe benches. Set up dedicated raw board, glass containers, fresh towels. Wash hands.
  • 32. Chicken: pat 2.0kg bird dry, rub with herb-garlic paste, put onion in cavity. Glass dish on bottom fridge shelf.
  • 33. Beef: cut 1.8kg chuck into 5cm cubes, sear in pressure cooker, add aromatics, spices, tinned tomatoes, stock, ACV. Pressure cook high 45 min, natural release 15 min. Shred.
  • 34. Pork: cut 2.2kg shoulder into 4 pieces, rub with sugar and spices. Sear, add stock and ACV, pressure cook high 70 min, natural release 15 min. Shred, toss with 600ml BBQ sauce.
  • 35. Butter chicken thighs: dice 1.5kg into 3cm chunks, toss with spices, oil, salt on 2 trays.
  • 36. Pasta-bake thighs: rub 1.0kg whole thighs with oil, garlic powder, oregano, salt on 1 tray.
  • 37. Lamb patties: mix 1.2kg lamb mince, onion, garlic, panko, egg, parsley, oregano, salt. Shape 18 patties (~80g).
  • 38. Nuggets: mix 1.2kg chicken mince, carrot, zucchini, egg, panko, spices. Shape 40 nuggets (~35g), roll in polenta.
  • 39. Sanitise all tools, boards and benches.

    • Stage E: Sequential Oven Queue, Cooling & Pack-Down

  • 40. Oven runs (fan-forced): - Run 1 (200°C, 30–35 min): sweet potato wedges ×2 trays + diced sweet potato. - Run 2 (200°C, 25 min): corn cobs. - Run 3 (180°C, 22 min): muffins. - Run 4 (180°C, 30 min): crumble bar tin + cluster tray (20 min). - Run 5 (180°C, 12 min/round): cookies. - Run 6 (210°C, 14 min): scones. - Run 7 (200°C, 18 min): fritters (mash diced sweet potato with corn, eggs, flour, baking powder, spring onions, cumin, salt; shape 32 patties). - Run 8 (190°C, 14 min): nuggets. - Run 9 (190°C, 16 min): lamb patties. - Run 10 (200°C, 22–25 min): butter chicken thighs + pasta-bake thighs.
  • 41. Assemble salsa with corn kernels, diced tomatoes, spring onions, parsley, ACV, oil, cumin, salt.
  • 42. Stir cooked butter chicken thighs into sauce, simmer 10 min. Dice pasta thighs. Thicken pork sauce with cornflour slurry. Reserve 500ml BBQ sauce in jar.
  • 43. Fill 250ml lemonade bottles (8 for freezer, leave headspace).
  • 44. Cool everything on racks. Pack fridge and freezer allocations with date labels.

Daily Usage Guide (Sunday to Saturday)

Sunday: Herb-Garlic Roast Chicken Feast

  1. Roast chicken at 200°C for 70–75 min. Roast parboiled potatoes in hot oil with rosemary for 50 min. Roast carrot batons with maple and thyme on the lower shelf.
  2. Steam green beans and peas. Reheat gravy base with pan juices.
  3. Dessert: Warm strawberry crumble clusters with compote and custard.

Lunch Rescue (Monday): Chicken and slaw wraps with leftover roast chicken, slaw, and aioli.

Monday: Slow-Braised Beef Tacos

  1. Reheat braised beef with water. Warm tortillas in a dry pan.
  2. Build with black beans, corn salsa, dressed slaw, pickled red onion, and crema. Serve with sweet potato wedges.

Lunch Rescue (Tuesday): Beef and bean burritos toasted in a pan.

Tuesday: Pulled Pork BBQ Rolls

  1. Reheat pulled pork gently. Toast rolls.
  2. Layer pork, dressed slaw, and pickles, with BBQ sauce on the side. Serve with sweet potato wedges and frozen peas.

Lunch Rescue (Wednesday): Pulled pork and slaw wraps.

Wednesday: Creamy Cauliflower & Chicken Baked Pasta

  1. Cook pasta 2 min short. Steam broccoli florets. Mix pasta with cauliflower sauce, thawed thigh pieces, broccoli, and spinach.
  2. Top with panko and oil. Bake at 200°C for 15 min. Serve with cucumber sticks.

Lunch Rescue (Thursday): Nugget plates with wedges and dip.

Thursday: Mild Butter Chicken with Rice

  1. Reheat butter chicken with stock/water and peas. Reheat basmati rice.
  2. Warm tortillas as quick roti. Serve with diced cucumber in crema.

Lunch Rescue (Friday): Butter chicken wraps with rice and slaw.

Friday: Lamb & Parsley Burgers

  1. Reheat lamb patties at 190°C for 10 min. Toast buns.
  2. Build with aioli, cos, tomato, and pickled red onion. Serve with reheated wedges and slaw.

Lunch Rescue (Saturday): Snack plate with fritters, nuggets, scone, and dip.

Saturday: Freezer-Raid Loaded Nachos

  1. Reheat thawed beef with black beans. Spread corn chips on trays.
  2. Top with beef, beans, and salsa. Bake at 200°C for 8 min. Finish with crema and cos.
